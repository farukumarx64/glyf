# Replacement public-brand research

Reviewed **2026-11-21**, for the previously confirmed worldwide launch ambition. The user requested research into a replacement public brand after asking why Mosaic was a release concern.

**Earlier recommendation: Folioweft**, pronounced “FOH-lee-oh-weft.” This is the recommended candidate for adoption and further clearance, not a claim of worldwide availability or a recorded user decision to rename the project. That research did not rename the product. The subsequent user decision below supersedes this recommendation.

## User-selected identity — 2026-11-22

The owner selected **Glyf**, proposed **`.glyf`**, and supplied **One format. Infinite expressions.** The owner subsequently approved completing the rename before adoption: current files declare `"glyf": "0.1.0"`, APIs/types and fixtures use Glyf names, and the obsolete `mosaic` marker is rejected. The rc.2 compatibility approach is superseded; [compatibility](compatibility.md#glyf-name-and-file-extension) documents the boundary. Folioweft was a research recommendation, not the adopted brand.

This selection does **not** establish an uncrowded name. Primary-source checks found:

- [glyf.ink](https://www.glyf.ink/terms) describes an existing browser/Android tool for turning images and video into text/pattern art, with processing on the user's device. Its terms assert rights in its name and materials. This is relevant adjacent creative-software usage, not a finding of infringement.
- [Glyf: Draw to Launch](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quarkstudio.glyf&hl=en_US) is an existing Android app listed by Quark studios.
- Microsoft's [OpenType specification](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/typography/opentype/spec/glyf) defines the `glyf` table for TrueType glyph outlines. That is an internal table tag, not proof of a `.glyf` standalone file extension. The [UFO GLIF specification](https://unifiedfontobject.org/versions/ufo3/glyphs/glif/) uses the similar but distinct `.glif` extension.

Exact searches for `".glyf" "file extension"` and `"One format. Infinite expressions."` did not establish an existing standalone `.glyf` document format or exact slogan match in the returned results. Search coverage is incomplete; neither result establishes availability. No Glyf-specific trademark-register, domain-registration, package-namespace or comprehensive language/similarity clearance has been completed. The earlier Folioweft register/namespace findings do not transfer to Glyf. The private local package name `glyf` reserves nothing publicly.

The public-name choice is recorded and implemented; worldwide clearance and extension-registry coverage remain open. No domain, handle, trademark or package was registered, and the existing GitHub repository remains `farukumarx64/mosaic`.

## Why move away from Mosaic?

[UW/CMU Mosaic](https://idl.uw.edu/mosaic/spec/) already describes interactive applications using JSON/YAML specifications with data, parameters, plots, inputs and layouts. That is unusually close to this project's visual-document format and viewer. The concrete concern is user/developer confusion and difficulty distinguishing the projects in search and documentation. This establishes overlapping use, not an infringement finding.

The earlier [public-release screen](public-release-checks.md#naming-and-namespace-findings) records additional Mosaic uses and the occupied unscoped npm name. Those findings make a distinctive compound name preferable to another Mosaic variant. A package-name conflict alone would not require changing the public brand; the close visualization/specification overlap is the stronger reason.

## Remaining coverage and decisions

The scope is a preliminary collision screen. [USPTO guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching) calls for considering similar marks and related goods/services, and [WIPO](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database) recommends relevant national/regional searches in addition to its participating collections. TMview itself states that its search tools are not official registers and their information has no legal effect.

- **WIPO's Global Brand Database was not searched directly.** Its web response presented a verification challenge. No challenge was bypassed and no absence-of-record claim is made.
- **Worldwide clearance is incomplete.** This work did not independently search every national/regional register, verify collection freshness, check all phonetic/translated variants or investigate all local/unregistered use. In particular, no direct Nigerian national-register review is established by this report. A qualified trademark professional should scope and assess the selected brand against the actual software/downloadable-viewer and any intended hosted-service goods/services, documenting jurisdictions and unresolved gaps.
- **Brand adoption is still a user decision.** Folioweft is the recommendation from this research. A subsequent rename should update public-facing wording and artifact/package naming coherently, while preserving existing `.mosa` compatibility unless a separate format change is authorized.
- **Domain/package findings are provisional.** A 404 response is not a reservation, price quote, legal right or guarantee that registration/publication will succeed. Social handles and non-`.com` domains were not comprehensively screened.
- **The `.mosa` extension review remains separate.** This research does not complete the missing file-format registry checks or choose a replacement suffix. Existing candidate artifacts retain their reviewed bytes and Mosaic labels.

Public naming has a concrete proposed replacement and stronger registry-search evidence, but the public-release naming gate is not marked cleared. The earlier pre-release implementation was committed as `c83b5b5`; this report does not alter that candidate's application, format or artifacts.
